Questions and answers

Will I receive a canvas, kit or finished ornament?

No. This is a digital pattern (PDF and PNG files). Nothing is shipped.

Are the pictures photos of stitched pieces?

No. The pictures of finished ornaments and key fobs are digital previews made from the chart, so you can see how it will look. The chart pages shown are the real pages of the PDF.

How do I download my pattern?

After payment, open etsy.com in a web browser on your phone or computer and go to You > Purchases > Download files. The Etsy app can't download digital files. You can download again at any time.

I didn't get the email.

Check your spam folder and Gmail's Promotions tab. Your files are always under Purchases on etsy.com.

Which mesh should I buy?

Most patterns include both 13 and 18 mesh; a made-to-order chart is charted for the one mesh you choose. 13 mesh is bigger and quicker to stitch; 18 mesh is finer and gives the classic 3" ornament.

How do I print at the right size?

Print the true-size page at 100% or "Actual size", with "Fit to page" off. Measure the 1-inch square before you trace. US Letter and A4 paper both work.

Can I use wool or pearl cotton?

The thread list gives DMC six-strand floss numbers. DMC Pearl Cotton uses the same numbers for the shades it comes in. Wool matches aren't listed because there is no reliable color conversion.

When will my personalized chart arrive?

Within 1–2 business days, through your Etsy order. Please check your spelling at checkout: I chart it exactly as typed.

What should I type for a personalized chart?

Exactly what you want stitched, with the spelling and capitals you want. For two lines, write them as you want them split. Signal-flag designs use the letters A to Z and the numbers 0 to 9.

Can I see my personalized design first?

Yes: write "preview please" in the personalization box and I'll send a picture before the final files.

Can I sell what I stitch?

The chart is for personal use and gifts, so please don't sell pieces made from it.

Can I have a canvas painted from my chart?

Yes. Paint it yourself, or ask a canvas painter to paint one canvas for you to stitch. Please don't sell painted canvases of the design.

Can you chart my school, team, sorority or club logo?

I'm sorry, no: those names and logos belong to their owners. I'm happy to chart a name, initials, a date or a boat name instead.

Something is wrong with my file. Can I get a refund?

Digital files can't be returned, but you're never stuck with one that doesn't work: message me through Etsy and I'll fix or replace it within 1 business day, or refund you if I can't. Bought the same chart twice by mistake? Message me within 7 days and I'll refund the duplicate.

Another question? Message the shop on Etsy.
