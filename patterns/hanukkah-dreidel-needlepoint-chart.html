Hanukkah Dreidel

Hanukkah Dreidel Needlepoint Pattern PDF, Bag Charm and Ornament Chart for 13 or 18 Mesh

Instant download

Buy on Etsy

Hanukkah Dreidel needlepoint pattern (PDF): A royal blue dreidel with the Hebrew letter shin in gold, charted two ways: a dreidel-shaped bag charm with a white border and a royal blue edge, and a 3-inch round on blue and white gingham for an ornament or a gift tag.

Instant download. A digital pattern: a PDF chart, a ZIP of 6 PNG files (true size for 13 and 18 mesh, and Stitchly-ready), and the 2 Stitchly-ready PNGs on their own. Not a painted canvas or kit; nothing is shipped.

How to download

Open etsy.com in a web browser (the Etsy app can't download files) > You > Purchases > Download files.

You get

  • 16-page PDF: color chart, printer-friendly symbol chart, thread list, how-to, and true-size pages for tracing
  • ZIP: 4 true-size PNGs (13 and 18 mesh for each design); 2 Stitchly-ready PNGs, 1 pixel = 1 stitch (one per design); a DMC color list per design to check the import against; and step-by-step Stitchly import notes
  • The 2 Stitchly-ready PNGs as downloads of their own too (the same files as in the ZIP): save them straight to your phone, no unzipping.
  • Both 13 and 18 mesh in one download: no need to buy twice.
  • A DMC shopping list for both designs: every floss number once, with skein estimates for the whole set, on one page; each design's own thread list too.

Size (stitched area)

  • Bag Charm: 1.6" x 2.2" on 18 mesh, 2.2" x 3.1" on 13 mesh (28 x 40 stitches)
  • Round Ornament: 3.0" x 3.0" on 18 mesh, 4.2" x 4.2" on 13 mesh (54 x 54 stitches)

You'll need

  • Blank 13 or 18 mesh canvas (the PDF gives the size to cut)
  • 10 DMC floss colors (numbers and skein estimates are in the PDF)
  • A tapestry needle; acrylic paint and a canvas marker if you paint your own canvas

Skill: Confident beginner (10 colors per design and a few small details)

Using the Stitchly app

  • Each Stitchly-ready PNG is 1 pixel = 1 stitch; its size and number of DMC colors are in its file name.
  • In Stitchly, choose Convert a photo and type in that size and color count. Step-by-step notes are in the download.
  • It's a PNG image, not a .stitchly project file. Stitchly is a separate app, sold separately, and importing may need its full version. This shop isn't affiliated with it.
