Name Christmas Ornament Set of 5

Personalized Name Christmas Ornament Needlepoint Patterns, Family Set of 5, Made to Order PDF Charts

Made to order

Buy on Etsy

Personalized Name Christmas Ornament Set of 5 needlepoint patterns, made to order (PDF): One ornament chart per name, each with a little Santa hat over the name in white in falling snow, on evergreen with a red border, each a 4-inch round on 18 mesh (5.5 inches on 13): up to 5 names in one order, all charted to match. One for each grandchild, or the whole family. The photo shows a full set of 5 names.

Made to order. I chart your text for your mesh and send the PDF through your Etsy order, usually within 1–2 business days. This is a digital pattern: nothing is shipped, and it is not a painted canvas or kit.

How to download

Open etsy.com in a web browser (the Etsy app can't download files) > You > Purchases > your order, once I've sent your chart.

You get

  • PDF charted for your text: color chart, printer-friendly symbol chart, thread list, how-to, and true-size pages for tracing
  • For each name: a true-size PNG for your mesh, plus a Stitchly-ready PNG (1 pixel = 1 stitch) with step-by-step Stitchly import notes
  • A DMC thread list: every floss number, color name and skein estimate on one page.

Size (sample shown; your ornament is this size on your mesh)

  • Name Christmas Ornament Set (sample: Ava): 4.0" x 4.0" on 18 mesh, 5.5" x 5.5" on 13 mesh (72 x 72 stitches)
  • Name Christmas Ornament Set (sample: Kate): 4.0" x 4.0" on 18 mesh, 5.5" x 5.5" on 13 mesh (72 x 72 stitches)
  • Name Christmas Ornament Set (sample: Christine): 4.0" x 4.0" on 18 mesh, 5.5" x 5.5" on 13 mesh (72 x 72 stitches)

You'll need

  • Blank 13 or 18 mesh canvas (the PDF gives the size to cut)
  • DMC floss in the colors on your chart's thread list (the samples use 5)
  • A tapestry needle; acrylic paint and a canvas marker if you paint your own canvas

Skill: Confident beginner (the sample: 5 colors per design and a few small details)

What to enter when you order

  • Names, separated by commas (up to 5), up to 60 characters: Up to 10 letters a name, e.g. Ava, Kate, Christine. Script if all fit; if one needs serif or block capitals, all match.
  • Mesh: pick your mesh from the list (18 or 13).

Lettering: your text is stitched in script when it fits; a longer one in serif letters, and the longest in block capitals (capital letters only).

Using the Stitchly app

  • Each name gets its own Stitchly-ready PNG, charted for your text and sent with your PDF.
  • It's a PNG image, not a .stitchly project file. Stitchly is a separate app, sold separately, and importing may need its full version. This shop isn't affiliated with it.

License

Personal use: paint and stitch it for yourself and as gifts. Please don't share or resell the files, sell canvases painted from them, or use them for mass production.

Colors on screen are approximate; check them against a DMC color card. Because this is a digital download it can't be returned, but if anything is wrong with your file, message me and I'll make it right.
