Guides

Mesh, sizes and strands

Most patterns include both 13 and 18 mesh; a made-to-order chart is charted for the one mesh you choose. 13 mesh is bigger and quicker to stitch; 18 mesh is finer and gives the classic 3" ornament.

How big it stitches up

A chart counts stitches. Its finished size is the number of stitches divided by the mesh, the canvas's stitches per inch, so the same chart stitches up larger on 13 mesh than on 18. Every listing gives its finished size on each mesh it is charted for.

Chart13 mesh18 mesh
A round ornament (54 x 54 stitches)4.2" x 4.2"3.0" x 3.0"
A key fob (22 x 90 stitches)1.7" x 6.9"1.2" x 5.0"

Strands of floss

The thread list gives DMC six-strand floss numbers. For tent stitch, separate the strands and stitch with 6 to 8 together on 13 mesh, 4 together on 18 mesh; add a strand if canvas shows through.

CanvasStrands of six-strand floss, tent stitch
10 mesh8 to 10
12 mesh6 to 8
13 mesh6 to 8
14 mesh6 or 7
18 mesh4

The skein counts on the thread list are estimates, rounded up, for DMC six-strand floss (8.7 yd skeins) in basketweave.

Needle

Use a tapestry needle; #18 for 13 mesh, #20 or #22 for 18 mesh are the usual sizes.

Can I use wool or pearl cotton?

The thread list gives DMC six-strand floss numbers. DMC Pearl Cotton uses the same numbers for the shades it comes in. Wool matches aren't listed because there is no reliable color conversion.
