How to read the chart and print the true-size page

Every pattern is a PDF you download, with a color chart, a black-and-white symbol chart, a DMC thread list and true-size pages for your mesh, so you can paint your own canvas or stitch straight from the chart.

Reading the chart

One square is one tent stitch over one canvas intersection. Heavy lines mark every 10 stitches and the red arrows mark the center. On the symbol chart, blank squares are the background color named in the key.

Which pages to print

  • To paint a canvas: print the true-size page for your mesh at 100%, trace it onto the canvas, then paint, using the color chart to settle any unclear square.
  • To stitch from the chart: print the color chart, or the symbol chart to save ink, plus the thread list.
  • On a tablet: no printing needed. Zoom into the color chart; the bookmarks list every section.

Printing the true-size page

A true-size page is the design at the size it stitches up on your mesh, to trace onto canvas. Each one carries a 1-inch square to check the print against.

  • Print at 100% / Actual size, not "Fit to page". US Letter and A4 paper both work at 100%.
  • Measure the 1-inch square on a true-size page before you trace.
  • Colors on screen and paper are approximate: match threads against a DMC color card.
