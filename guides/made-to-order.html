How a made-to-order chart works

A made-to-order pattern is charted for your text after you order. This is how the listings describe it.

How it works

Made to order. I chart your text for your mesh and send the PDF through your Etsy order, usually within 1–2 business days. This is a digital pattern: nothing is shipped, and it is not a painted canvas or kit.

How to download

Open etsy.com in a web browser (the Etsy app can't download files) > You > Purchases > your order, once I've sent your chart.

When will my personalized chart arrive?

Within 1–2 business days, through your Etsy order. Please check your spelling at checkout: I chart it exactly as typed.

What should I type for a personalized chart?

Exactly what you want stitched, with the spelling and capitals you want. For two lines, write them as you want them split. Signal-flag designs use the letters A to Z and the numbers 0 to 9.

Can I see my personalized design first?

Yes: write "preview please" in the personalization box and I'll send a picture before the final files.

For signal-flag names, preview your name in signal flags first.
