About the shop

Buoys & Bobbins NDLPT makes digital needlepoint patterns: Christmas and Hanukkah ornaments, birth flowers, and names, stockings and key fobs charted to order. Buoys for the coast, bobbins for the thread.

Every pattern is a PDF you download, with a color chart, a black-and-white symbol chart, a DMC thread list and true-size pages for your mesh, so you can paint your own canvas or stitch straight from the chart. Most patterns include both 13 and 18 mesh; a made-to-order chart is charted for the one mesh you choose. Nothing is shipped.

Colors are matched to real DMC floss numbers, and every chart passes automated checks for stray single stitches, colors too close to tell apart and text too small to read before it's listed.

The pictures of finished pieces in my listings are digital previews made from the chart, not photos of stitched samples. The chart pages you see are the real pages of the PDF.

Personalized charts are made to order and sent through your Etsy order within 1–2 business days.

Questions? Message me through Etsy. I reply within 24 hours.
