License and terms

Patterns are bought on Etsy. These are the shop's terms for what you can do with a chart, and what happens if something is wrong with a file.

License

Your chart is for your personal use. You may stitch it, and paint your own canvas from it, for yourself and as gifts. Please don't share, copy or resell the files or the charts, sell canvases painted from them, or use them for mass production. Thank you for supporting a small shop.

Each chart PDF also prints this line: © 2026 Buoys & Bobbins NDLPT. For personal use only: please do not share, resell or mass-produce this chart.

Refunds and exchanges

Because digital files can't be returned, I don't accept returns or exchanges once a chart has been delivered. But you're never stuck with a file that doesn't work:

- A problem with a file (it won't open, a page is missing, something doesn't match the listing): message me and I'll fix or replace it within 1 business day. If I can't fix it, I'll refund you in full. - Bought the same chart twice by mistake: message me within 7 days and I'll refund the duplicate. - Personalized charts: you can cancel for a full refund any time before I deliver your files. After delivery, the preview and free revision in "Personalized charts" apply.

Buyers in the EU and UK: an instant download is delivered right away, and once you have agreed to immediate delivery and the download is available, the 14-day right of withdrawal no longer applies. For personalized charts you can cancel before delivery, as above. This doesn't affect any other rights you have under your local law.

Personalized charts

Type your text exactly as you want it stitched in the personalization box (spelling, capitals and spaces). Pick the mesh.

Turnaround: I chart your text and send the files through Etsy within 1 to 2 business days. You'll get an email from Etsy when they are ready to download.

Preview: if you'd like to see your design before the final files, write "preview please" in the personalization box. I'll send a picture in Etsy Messages first and deliver as soon as you say it looks right.

One free revision: if something isn't right (a spelling you changed your mind about, a different color palette from the listing), message me within 14 days of delivery and I'll send one revised chart at no charge. A mistake on my side is always fixed, no matter how many tries it takes.

If your text doesn't fit: some sizes hold only so many letters. If yours doesn't fit, I'll message you within 1 business day with the options: a shorter version, a two-line layout or a larger size. If none of them works for you, I'll refund your order in full.

What I can't chart: team, school, college, sorority, fraternity, club or brand names, logos, characters and anything else that belongs to someone else. I'll let you know and refund you if an order asks for one.
