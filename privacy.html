Privacy

This website

  • The site sets no cookies and has no analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No forms and no sign-ups. The signal-flag previewer runs in your browser: what you type stays on your device and is not saved.
  • Every font, script and picture comes from this site itself. Links to Etsy take you to Etsy.
  • Like any website, the service that hosts these pages may keep technical logs of visits, such as IP addresses; its own privacy policy covers them.

When you buy on Etsy

I use the information Etsy gives me with your order (your name, your Etsy username and any personalization text) only to make and deliver your order and to answer your messages. I never add you to a mailing list, share your information with anyone else or use it for advertising without asking you first.

Personalization text is kept on my computer only while it's needed for your order and any revision, and deleted within 90 days. My business records keep only what was made (format and date), not your text. Etsy's own privacy policy covers what Etsy stores.

To see or delete what I hold about you, message me through Etsy.
