About the flags

The International Code of Signals gives every letter from A to Z its own flag and every number from 0 to 9 its own pennant. There is no flag for a space or for punctuation, so the preview spells only letters and numbers.

Nothing you type here leaves your device: the previewer runs in your browser, and this site has no forms and sets no cookies.
